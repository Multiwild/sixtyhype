Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

sixtyhype runs a small internal tool ("the App") that prepares short gaming clips and publishes them to the sixtyhype accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. The App is used only by the sixtyhype team to manage its own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data the App processes

  • Platform access tokens for the sixtyhype accounts, granted via the official OAuth login of TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta. They are stored encrypted on our own system and used only to upload videos and read basic account information (username, avatar) of these accounts.
  • Public clip metadata from Twitch (clip title, streamer name, view count) to select and credit clips.

The App does not collect data from visitors or viewers, does not use cookies or tracking on this website, and does not sell or share data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when access is revoked in the respective platform's settings or when the App is discontinued. You can request deletion of any data related to you by email.

Third-party platforms

Content is published on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, whose own privacy policies apply.
